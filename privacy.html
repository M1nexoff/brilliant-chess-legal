Privacy Policy

Brilliant Chess · Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Brilliant Chess mobile app and its backend (“Brilliant Chess”, “we”, “us”) collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have — including how to delete your account and data.

1. Data we collect

Account & sign-in

You can sign in with any one of three methods. We only collect what that method provides:

  • Google Sign-In — your Google account identifier and the email/display name Google returns, used to create and recognise your account.
  • Email one-time code (OTP) — your email address, used to send the sign-in code and to identify your account.
  • Telegram Mini App — your Telegram user id, used to auto-sign-in inside Telegram.

Every account has a display name (shown to other players) and an in-app username. We do not ask for or store a password for normal players.

Gameplay

  • Your ratings (bullet, blitz, rapid) and win/loss/draw statistics.
  • Your game history — opponents, results, time controls, timestamps, and the moves played.
  • In-game chat messages you send, and reports you file about other players.
  • Your friends list and friend requests.
  • Watching live games — while a game is in progress, your friends and, for the top-rated games, other players can watch it. They see both players’ display names, ratings and the moves.
  • Founder badge — to award it to early players, we check the date your sign-in account was created in Firebase Authentication.

Technical & security

  • IP address — processed server-side to rate-limit abusive traffic and protect the service. It is not part of your public profile.
  • Push-notification token — if you enable notifications, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token so we can send you game and friend notifications. It is removed when you sign out on that device or delete your account.
  • Game review — the Stockfish engine analyses your games entirely on your device; your games are not sent anywhere for review. The first time you use it (and if you turn on Deep analysis) the app downloads the engine’s data files from our server.

Diagnostics & analytics (via Firebase)

  • Crash & ANR diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics) — crash stack traces, app-not- responding events and device/OS metadata, so we can fix stability problems.
  • Analytics (Firebase Analytics), including your device’s Advertising ID — aggregate usage events that help us understand which features are used and improve the app.

3. Who we share data with (third parties)

We do not sell your personal data. We share data with the service providers we rely on:

  • Google / Firebase — Google Sign-In (authentication), Firebase Crashlytics (crash & ANR reporting) and Firebase Analytics (usage analytics, Advertising ID). Their handling of this data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy. Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers push notifications and Firebase Remote Config tells the app which server to use.
  • Brevo — sends the one-time sign-in code to your email address if you sign in with email.
  • Cloudflare — the connection between the app and our server runs through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and request data to deliver and protect it.
  • Telegram — if you choose to sign in through the Telegram Mini App. We also receive internal service alerts on Telegram, which include the display name and username of newly registered players.

We may also disclose data where required by law.

4. How long we keep it (retention)

  • Account & gameplay data — kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your identifying data is erased (see below).
  • Game records — after deletion, past games are retained for your opponents’ history with your player reference anonymised to “Deleted user”. They no longer identify you.
  • Sign-in codes & IP-based rate-limit state — held only transiently (minutes) and then discarded.
  • Crash & analytics data — retained by Firebase according to Google’s retention settings.

5. Your right to delete your account and data

You can delete your account and personal data at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete account.
  • On the web: use our account deletion form if you no longer have the app installed.

When you delete your account, we permanently remove it from our systems. Specifically, we:

  • erase your email, display name and username;
  • delete your linked sign-in identity — including the Google / Firebase Authentication account and the Telegram identifier connected to you — so that sign-in is removed from Firebase Authentication and no longer appears there;
  • remove your registered push-notification (FCM) device tokens, so your devices stop receiving notifications from us;
  • delete your friends, friend requests, challenges, activity feed, reports and in-game chat messages;
  • sign you out on all devices.

Past games are kept for your opponents’ history with your player reference anonymised to “Deleted user”; they no longer identify you. Crash and analytics data already sent to Firebase is retained by Google under its own retention settings.

6. Children

Brilliant Chess is not directed to children under the age required by your country’s law. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
